## Core Tracking & Logging

- [ ] **Barcode Scanner**: Enable quick food logging via camera scanning of product barcodes.
- [ ] **Custom Food Creation**: Allow users to define custom foods with specific name, serving size, macro breakdown, TEF, and full nutrient profile.
- [ ] **Custom Recipe Builder**: Create recipes by combining multiple foods with proportional serving sizes for easy logging.
- [ ] **Recipe Importer**: Scrape ingredients and instructions from external URLs to automatically build recipes.
- [ ] **Meal Templates**: Create re-usable meal templates (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks) for faster logging.
- [ ] **"Quick Add" Single Entry**: streamlining logging flow to immediately open details dialog.
- [ ] **Recent Saved Searches**: Persist recent search queries for quick access.
- [ ] **Favorite Foods**: Allow users to mark foods as favorites for rapid retrieval.
- [ ] **Exploded Recipe View**: Option to display individual ingredients of a logged recipe directly in the diary.
- [ ] **Repeat Items & Scheduler**: Automate logging for recurring items (e.g., daily coffee).
- [ ] **Copy Meals**: Quickly duplicate meals from previous days ("Copy Yesterday's Breakfast").
- [ ] **Photos in Diary**: Attach photos to specific diary entries for visual tracking.
- [ ] **Offline Mode**: Enable full logging capabilities without internet access, syncing when back online.

## Advanced Nutrition & Analytics

- [ ] **Smart Nutrient Suggestions**: "The Oracle" - suggest specific foods to fill remaining nutrient gaps for the day.
- [ ] **Nutrient Balances**: Track key ratios including Omega-3:6, Zinc:Copper, Potassium:Sodium, and Calcium:Magnesium.
- [ ] **Dynamic Energy Settings**: Calculate burn based on a "Sedentary" baseline plus actual synced activity, rather than a static multiplier.
- [ ] **Thermic Effect of Food (TEF)**: Toggle to include TEF (approx. 10% of intake) in total energy expenditure calculations.
- [ ] **Pregnancy & Lactation Modes**: Automatically adjust DRIs and energy targets for specific life stages.
- [ ] **Net Carbs Region Toggle**: Global setting for "European Mode" (fiber already subtracted) vs "US Mode".
- [ ] **Nutrient Exclusion Settings**: granular control to exclude calories from specific sources like Sugar Alcohols for specific goals.
- [ ] **Alcohol Tracking**: Specialized tracking for alcohol with options for separate macro accounting or calorie-only logging.
- [ ] **Glucose & Ketone Tracking**: Log blood glucose/ketones and calculate GKI (Glucose Ketone Index).
- [ ] **Advanced Biometric Correlations**: Charts comparing nutrient intake against weight, sleep, and recovery metrics.
- [ ] **Health Scores**: Aggregate complex nutrient data into simplified scores (Immunity, Bone Health, Antioxidant status).
- [ ] **Target Scheduler**: Set different macro targets for specific days (e.g., High Carb Weekends, Training vs Rest days).

## Lifestyle & Biometrics

- [ ] **Device Integrations**: Sync activity, sleep, and biometrics from Apple Health, Google Fit, Oura, Fitbit, and Garmin.
- [ ] **Weight Logging**: Track daily weight changes.
- [ ] **Body Composition Logging**: Track body fat %, measurements (adipometer, tape), and scans (DEXA).
- [ ] **Sleep Logging**: Track sleep duration and quality.
- [ ] **Activity Logging**: Log exercises and non-exercise activity thermogenesis (NEAT).
- [ ] **Water Tracking**: Simple hydration logging.
- [ ] **Intermittent Fasting Timer**: Integrated timer and tracker for fasting windows.
- [ ] **Biometrics & Labs**: Log clinical markers like Cholesterol (LDL/HDL), Triglycerides, etc.
- [ ] **Custom Biometrics**: Allow users to define and track custom numeric or boolean metrics.
- [ ] **Weight Loss Predictions**: Project weight trends based on current caloric deficit/surplus.
